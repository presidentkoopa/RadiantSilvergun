=======================================================================
rs_13 -- MONSTER SIGNATURE SHORTLIST (exotic affix lane, rs_11 EXOTIC)
=======================================================================
Survey of every projectile class in zscript/monsters/monsterfx/ (all 16
files read in full), ranked as candidates for "your bullets become a
monster's own ammunition" signature affixes.

THE PROVEN PATTERN (read these before building anything):
  * TFLV_Upgrade_RS_Bonecaller  (zscript/gunbonsai/rs_upgrades/RS_Upgrade_Slate.zsc)
  * RS_AffixBoneTracer          (zscript/weapons/weaponfx/RS_FX_AffixParts.zs)
An adapter is NEVER the monster class itself. It is a new class that
extends RS_BallisticFired (rides the whole pipeline: SetupStats exact
damage, trails, seek/pierce fields, feedback) wearing the monster
round's sprite + sounds + decal, installed via wpn.AffixProjectile +
wpn.AffixFireSound. The ONE exception: anything that BOUNCES must be a
plain Actor extending RS_AffixPartActor -- FastProjectile cannot bounce,
engine limitation (this is why RS_AffixIceOrb exists). So "base class"
below tells you which adapter chassis the signature needs, and the
monster class's baked behaviors tell you what to re-author on it.

AVAILABILITY LEGEND (every sprite/sound below was VERIFIED, not assumed):
  [IWAD]     vanilla doom2 asset -- zero risk.
  [REPO]     physically in E:\RS_Main\sprites\ or defined in E:\RS_Main\SNDINFO.
  [CH-EXT]   ** NOT in repo, NOT vanilla -- resolves ONLY when the
             Colourful Hell pack is loaded. Sprite = INVISIBLE round
             without CH; sound = silent. Building a signature on a
             CH-EXT sprite means re-importing the art first. **

In-repo projectile sprites (complete list, from scanning sprites/):
  ICEY (frames A,B,C,F,G,H,I ONLY -- NO D/E, see rs_MASTER_FX_CATALOG;
  every monsterfx "ICEY CDE" death state is broken without CH),
  RIP1 (A-H), WASP (A,B rot + C,D), FRGO, ETHS, LFX1, AYPE, HFRY.
Everything else a monster round wears is IWAD or CH-EXT.

DISCREPANCY FLAG: RS_hk_projectiles.zs's Black-arsenal header claims
"Custom sprites ripped (STRS/FBRS/BAL3/MSLH/MTRL/BBOM)" -- NONE of those
six are in sprites/. They are CH-EXT. The comment is stale/aspirational.

Repo SNDINFO defines (relevant to these rounds): rs_fx_ice_cast/hit/
shatter/fly, rs_fx_impact_bullet, hq_dsfirsht, prox/beep, monster/dknmsl,
monster/dkndrt, butcher/melee, queen/fire, moloch/nailhitbleed,
fiend/bomb. Everything CH-flavored (ice/Cast, Ice/Hit2, fire/fire*,
Crack/*, Spell/*, Litn/*, holy*, Bomb/bounce, weapons/hominglaunch,
weapons/firex*, weapons/hellex, weapons/grenl*, spike/spiked, ...) is
[CH-EXT]. Standard fix: swap to rs_fx_ice_* / rs_fx_impact_bullet /
vanilla names in the adapter, exactly as Bonecaller and Cryo did.

DESIGN-COLLISION NOTE (rs_09 exclusion groups): Painter owns FIRE
identity, Cryo owns ICE identity, Bonecaller owns the FATB revenant
tracer. Ice/fire candidates below either fold into those affixes as
higher-level parts (the Cryo-mastery orb precedent) or join the same
identity exclusion group -- they must not ship as a free second fire/ice
identity. Flagged per-entry as "collides: Painter/Cryo/Bonecaller".
