================================================================================
   RS_39 -- THE VR WEAPON WHEEL: WHAT THE ENGINE DOES, WHAT RS ANSWERS
================================================================================

WHY THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The engine has a VR weapon wheel. It has had one since 2026-06-08, written by
iAmErmac and inherited with the DoomXR fork. Nobody on the RS side knew, because
NOTHING IS BOUND TO IT BY DEFAULT -- three console commands are registered and no
keybind exists for any of them, so out of the box it is invisible.

That matters twice over. It is the in-world weapon selector RS wanted and did not
have to build. And PyWeaponWheel-VR, which was in the load order, is a flatscreen
ZScript reimplementation of the same idea by the same author, with its own time
freeze and its own +attack/+use grab -- two wheels fighting over one trigger.
Drop the mod; the engine's is strictly better.


HOW TO SEE IT AT ALL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bind these in the OpenXR controls menu. They are console functions registered in
g_game.cpp, NOT buttonMap entries, so `ButtonDown` cannot query them:

    +vrweaponwheel           main-hand weapon ring
    +vroffhandweaponwheel    off-hand weapon ring
    +vrinvwheel              item ring

Options page: VR menu -> weapon wheel. Roughly thirty cvars, all `vr_wheel_*`.


WHAT IT DOES NOW (all verified 2026-08-07)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE RING PER HAND, BOTH AT ONCE. Hold both binds and both rings appear, each
worked by its own controller. Before commit 832274516c the wheel was a single
global and the second bind replaced the first.

THE RING PARKS WHERE IT BLOOMS. It reads the controller pose at open, converts
that point into HEAD-relative space, and stops following the hand. This is
deliberate and is what makes same-hand selection possible: a ring welded to a
wrist moves exactly as fast as the hand reaching for it, so that hand can never
arrive. `vr_wheel_leash` (default 0 = parked) lets it follow the wrist with a
dead region instead.

FOUR SELECTION MODES, `vr_wheel_selection_type`:
    0  touch      hand position inside an icon's grab radius (default)
    1  aim        angular delta from the pose the wheel opened at (RELATIVE)
    2  thumbstick that hand's stick points at an icon (ABSOLUTE)
    3  pointer    a ray from the hand struck against the wheel's plane

Mode 3 is the one to reach for. Pointing is angular, so it does not care how
fast the wheel moves or how far away it is; modes 0 and 1 both do. Mode 1 is
relative to wherever you happened to be standing when you pressed the bind.

TIME IS NOT THE WHEEL'S BUSINESS ANY MORE. It used to drive i_timescale and
I_FreezeTime directly. Removed in eadabfff81, along with vr_wheel_time_slow.
See EVENTS below.


THE HOOK RS ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    virtual String PlayerPawn.GetVRWheelInfo(Inventory item, int hand)

Declared in the engine's own player.zs. Called for the entry under the hand;
returns newline-separated lines, first drawn larger as a heading. `\c[Name]`
colour escapes are honoured (7898236977 -- before that they printed literally).
hand is 0 main, 1 off. Empty string means "engine, use your own fallback", which
is the tag and ammo counts.

RS answers it in zscript/systems/ui/RS_WheelInfo.zs (commit c14925a3), as an
`extend class VR_DualClassBase`. It carries only what decides WHICH GUN TO HOLD:

    name in tier colour  /  tier + promotion pips  /  level, damage, condition
    /  affix count  /  loaded + reserve

This is deliberately NOT the weapon sheet. The sheet (RS_Screens S1/S2, spec
rs_10) is read standing still with time to spare and shows everything. This is
read mid-fight, so rate of fire, velocity, crit, choke and the affix roster stay
on the sheet. Colour follows L4: tier on the name, condition band on condition,
nothing else coloured.

Panel placement is `vr_wheel_info` -- 0 off, 1 the ring's hub, 2 beside it.


EVENTS RS CAN LISTEN FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Netevents, so they arrive in NetworkProcess, which is where mods of this kind
already listen:

    vrwheel_open    arg1 type, arg2 hand, arg3 wheels open AFTER the change
    vrwheel_close   same

type: 1 main weapon, 2 offhand weapon, 3 inventory. hand: 0 main, 1 off.

ARG3 IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS. Act when it becomes 1 and undo when it reaches 0,
and two rings behave like one. Every path that drops a wheel emits a close --
ordinary release, replacing a wheel on the same hand, dying, changing level,
VRWheel_Reset -- so a listener never sees an open it will not get a close for.
That was the failure designed against: a time mod stuck slow because the wheel
vanished without saying so.

`vr_wheel_time_event` / `_off` fire an extra named event on the first open and
last close, for pointing straight at a mod without writing a shim.

BULLET-TIME-X, EXACTLY. Stock 4.3.3 (the copy in the DooM VR rotation folder)
exposes `bt_activate` and `bt_activate_dodge` in its KEYCONF and NOTHING ELSE.
NetworkProcess just sets btConsoleActive = true. There is no deactivate event,
and it spends adrenaline to start. So:

    vr_wheel_time_event "bt_activate"

works and will burn your meter every time you open the wheel, with no way to
stop it early. A shim on the RS side catching vrwheel_open/close and deciding
for itself is the better path, and is why the engine does not hardcode the mod.


THE RS-SHAPED TRAP IN THE WHEEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every RS class weapon is six real subclasses -- VR_Revolver plus VR_Revolver2..6,
three main-hand and three carrying +WEAPON.OFFHANDWEAPON -- and the base sets
+WEAPON.NOHANDSWITCH.

PlayerPawn.MoveWeaponToHand REFUSES OUTRIGHT when a weapon is NoHandSwitch and
belongs to the other hand (player.zs, the guard at the top). Before 832274516c
both rings listed all six, so half of each was dead: the wheel would highlight
an entry, play the confirm sound, and do nothing. The wheel now filters by
whether the move would actually be taken.

That fix created a second problem, fixed in 335a2b07db: a hand holding none of
its own identities has a genuinely EMPTY wheel, and an empty wheel used to open
anyway -- taking the trigger away and drawing no icons. It now refuses and plays
the invalid sound.


WHAT IS STILL MISSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The wheel decides its own contents by walking the engine's weapon slots. RS
cannot curate that list, and for a mod with six copies of every gun plus imported
arsenals it is going to get long and badly ordered. In rough order of value:

  1. FAVOURITES / SCRIPT-SUPPLIED ENTRIES. Same hook shape as GetVRWheelInfo,
     but returning WHICH entries and in what order. This is the big one.
  2. CATEGORIES ON ONE BIND. Three wheel types are three separate binds today.
     The stick is free in modes 0/1/3, so paging with it is cheap.
  3. MEMORY. VRWheel_Reset zeroes everything on close, so the ring never reopens
     where it was left and there is no flick-back-to-last-weapon.
  4. A FIST IN THE EMPTY HAND. vr_wheel_hide_hand_weapon hides the weapon in the
     hand holding the ring and puts nothing there. Swapping in a fist psprite is
     an RS decision, and RS already gets vrwheel_open to hang it on.

Reference the owner raised for where this should eventually land: Spell Wheel VR
(Skyrim SE, Nexus 47630). Nexus blocks automated fetches, so nobody has read the
feature list -- ask the owner rather than guessing.


ENGINE FACTS THAT WILL BITE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOT compile-verified against a full build. Every file touched was checked with
`cl /Zs` only. The OpenXR SDK for that lives at
E:\UZDXREMA\build-dxr\vcpkg\installed\x64-windows\include -- vk_openxrdevice.cpp
also needs -DHAVE_VULKAN or oxr_procs.h fails on the Vulkan PFN types.

NONE OF IT HAS BEEN RUN. Distances, panel size, haptic strength and the leash
radius are all derived from geometry, not measured in a headset.

The wheel uses OpenXR's AIM pose, never the GRIP pose. The grip paths are created
at vk_openxrdevice.cpp and never bound to anything. Aim pose sits forward of the
fist along the pointing ray, so touch mode's "hand position" is out past the
knuckles. This is a real suspect if reaching feels wrong.

VRWheel_GetTransform has no callers anywhere in src/ or wadsrc/. It and
VRWheelState::Transform are dead.

BB_RING and BB_BAR are enumerated billboard payloads that DO NOT DRAW -- only
BB_TEXTURE does, pending shaders. The C++ billboard helpers are static inside
vmthunks.cpp and are ZScript-only, so the wheel cannot use billboards at all
today and draws its own quads.
