IMPORTANT NOTE ON FOLDER CONTENTS
  "dakka" is NOT a pure effects folder. It is a complete extracted weapon mod:
  first-person weapon sprites, a full player skin with death/gib animation,
  pickup items and even a text graphic are mixed in with the real effects.
  Roughly 40% of the dakka folder is unusable as generic combat FX. Every such
  sequence is marked [NOT FX] below so a generator never picks it.

  Naming convention decoded (dakka): D**F = muzzle Flash (reusable),
  D**G = Gun / first-person weapon sprite (not reusable).
  e.g. DKSF = shotgun flash, DKSG = shotgun weapon. This pairing holds for
  DKSF/DKSG, DMNF/DMNG, DSSF/DSSG, PLNF/PLNC.

FILE FORMAT NOTES
  - Many files have NO extension (XPAC, BBAL, BBB1, R044, ZAP7, FLAR, LEN*).
    All of them are in fact ordinary PNGs, just mislabelled. All rendered fine.
    There is no raw Doom picture-format lump anywhere in these five folders.
  - 5 files (EXP1*, EXP2*, XPAC*, RR00*, SCPRZ0) are valid PNGs that the Python
    Pillow library refuses to open, so they were excluded from the AUTOMATED
    duplicate sweep. They were all inspected visually instead, and manually
    compared against RS_Main explosions. GZDoom will load them normally.
  - Doom `grAb` sprite-offset chunks are present on essentially everything,
    so in-game alignment is already baked in. The only exceptions, which would
    need offsets set by hand on import, are:
        FLARES/LENB, LENG, LENR, LENY  (no grAb at all)
        FLARES/LENS                    (1 of 2 frames only)

TYPE COLUMN
  animation = frames change over time (an effect developing)
  rotation  = one solid object seen from 8 angles (filenames like A1 / A2A8)
  mixed     = rotation frames for flight PLUS a separate animation tail
  static    = single frame

FOLDER: FLARES   (13 sequences, 27 files)
================================================================================

The flare folder resolves into THREE clean families. Within each family the
sequences are pure colour swaps of one another, and where a sequence has an A0
and a B0 frame, B0 is simply the same image at 240px instead of 256px -- a SCALE
variant, NOT an animation frame. Treat each family as one effect with a colour
parameter; that is exactly what a procedural generator wants.

--- FAMILY 1: dithered starburst (long 4-point spike + short rays + noisy halo)
SEQUENCES: FSO1 (2 frames, orange), LENS (2, A0 white / O0 orange),
           LEYS (5: A0 yellow, B0 cyan-blue, G0 green, O0 orange, R0 red)
  SIZE: 256x256   TYPE: static / colour set
  VISUAL: A brilliant point-source star with four long thin spikes and a spray
    of shorter rays, wrapped in a heavily dithered circular halo that fades to
    black at the rim. The dithering gives it a gritty, retro, non-smooth look.
  FLAVOR WORDS: starburst, radiant, nova, dazzle
  USE: muzzleflash, ambient, in-flight-trail
  NOTE: *** LARGELY ALREADY IN RS_MAIN. LEYSR0 is byte-identical to
    flares\RSF8R0; LENSA0 is byte-identical to flares\RSF3A0; FSO1 and LEYSO0
    score 1.7-2.4 (visually indistinguishable) against RSF8. Only genuinely new
    colours here are LEYSB0 (cyan) and LEYSG0 (green). ***

--- FAMILY 2: ringed lens flare (8-point star inside a lens ring)
SEQUENCES: L2NB (blue), L2NG (green), L2NR (red), L2NY (amber/yellow)
  FRAMES: 2 each (A0 256px, B0 240px -- scale variants)
  SIZE: 255x255 / 240x240   TYPE: static / colour set
  VISUAL: A smooth, clean 8-point star burst with a distinct thin circular lens
    ring drawn around it at about half radius, all sitting on a dark radial
    falloff disc. Much smoother and more "optical/photographic" than Family 1.
  FLAVOR WORDS: lensfire, halo, beacon, prism
  USE: muzzleflash, ambient, projectile-visual
  NOTE: GENUINELY NEW -- nearest RS_Main match scored 10.6-19.8 (not a dupe).
    The circular lens ring is the distinguishing feature and RS_Main has no
    flare with it. Best new find in this folder. Four ready-made colours makes
    it ideal for an element-keyed muzzle flash.

--- FAMILY 3: soft radial glow blob (no star, no rays)
SEQUENCES: LENB (blue), LENG (green), LENR (red-orange), LENW (white/grey),
           LENY (amber)
  FRAMES: 2 each except LENW (1). A0 256px, B0 240px -- scale variants.
  SIZE: 256x256 / 240x240   TYPE: static / colour set
  VISUAL: A featureless soft gradient sphere -- bright at the centre, falling
    smoothly to black at the edge. The classic additive light-blob used for
    glow, bloom and light halos.
  FLAVOR WORDS: glowcore, aura, lumen, softlight
  USE: ambient, in-flight-trail, projectile-visual
  NOTE: Conceptually overlaps RS_Main RSF0 (blue glow) / RSF5 (orange glow) /
    RSF6 (green glow) but NOT byte-identical -- the falloff radius and core
    brightness differ (scores 9.6-19.3). Redundant in role. LENW (white) is the
    most useful addition since it is colour-neutral and can be tinted at
    runtime. *** These four also LACK grAb offsets -- offsets must be set by
    hand if imported. ***

SEQUENCE: FLAR
  FRAMES: 1 (B0 only -- no A frame present)   SIZE: 96x96   TYPE: static
  VISUAL: A dense red radial burst with a hot yellow-white pinpoint core and
    short soft rays, on a dark red disc. Smaller and much denser than the
    256px flares -- feels hotter and more contained.
  FLAVOR WORDS: emberflare, hotshot, signal, cinder
  USE: muzzleflash, ambient, projectile-visual
  NOTE: The only flare at this size; the compact 96px footprint makes it the
    most practical of the flares for an actual in-world muzzle flash.
    Missing its A frame -- verify before use.

--- [NOT FX] WEAPON / PLAYER / PICKUP / UI SPRITES --------------------------

COVERAGE CAVEAT
  Five sequences -- EXP1, EXP2, XPAC, RR00 and SCPR -- are valid PNGs that the
  Pillow library refuses to open, so they could not be included in the AUTOMATED
  duplicate sweep. I inspected all five visually and manually compared EXP1/EXP2
  and XPAC against RS_Main's explosion sets (RSE0, RSE1, RSE3, RSE4). EXP1/EXP2
  share the general orange-fire-column idea with RSE0 but are clearly different
  art in a different aspect ratio (72x85 vertical, with a dark smoke collapse
  phase that RSE0 does not have). I judge them NOT duplicates, but that judgment
  is by eye, not by hash.

TOP RECOMMENDATIONS FOR THE GENERATOR

Highest value, no overlap with existing content:

  1. PROJECTILES\R044 -- black void sphere with white smoke tendrils. Nothing
     like it exists in RS_Main. An entire dark/void weapon family could be built
     and named off this one asset.
  2. dakka\SMK1 / SMK2 / SMK3 / SMK4 -- a complete heavy/light smoke set, four
     variants at matched 64x64, 16-18 frames each. Immediately usable and fills
     a real gap.
  3. dakka\DSSF -- twin-plume double-barrel muzzle flash. Specific, readable,
     and a natural naming hook.
  4. FLARES L2NB/L2NG/L2NR/L2NY -- ringed lens flare in four ready-made colours.
     The lens ring is the distinguishing feature and RS_Main has no flare with
     it. Ideal element-keyed muzzle flash.
  5. dakka\PLZM -- full plasma bolt lifecycle (projectile plus impact) in one
     14-frame sequence. One asset serves two FX roles.
  6. EXPLOSIONS\EXP1 + EXP2 -- the only true full-arc mushroom explosion with a
     settled smoke phase, and it ships as two hand-varied rolls.
  7. dakka\DFLM -- soft billowing napalm cloud, distinctly different from
     RS_Main's existing fire.
  8. PROJECTILES\RR00 -- crystalline shatter burst; the obvious cold/ice impact.
  9. dakka\MIS3 -- true 8-angle rotational missile. Rotational projectiles are
     rare and much better than billboards for physical ordnance.
 10. dakka\DKSF + DMNF -- large and small flame-crown muzzle flashes that work
     as a matched calibre-scaled pair.
