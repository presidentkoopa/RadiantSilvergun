1. WHAT IS OFFERED, BY TIER            [BUILT, verified 2026-08-04]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  On level-up, check the weapon's TIER:

    Cursed / Trash / Basic     0 sockets. Stat increase cards ONLY.
    Common and above           1..5 sockets. Stat cards AND a chance of
                               GunBonsai affixes.

  Sockets by tier -- RS_Roll.SocketsForTier(), the single source of truth:
    Cursed 0 · Trash 0 · Basic 0 · Common 1 · Uncommon 2 · Advanced 3 ·
    Designer 4 · Prototype 5

  SOCKETS ARE A BREADTH CAP, NOT A LEVEL CAP. They limit how many
  DISTINCT affixes a weapon holds. Re-picking a held affix to level it
  never costs a socket -- RS_Upgrade_Slate.zsc CanTake() returns true for
  an already-held class before it ever consults the count.

  Stat/combo cards deliberately do NOT gate on sockets, so a 0-socket
  weapon still gets a real offer rather than an empty one.


2. WHAT PROMOTION DOES TODAY           [BUILT, 2026-08-04]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Promotion drives BOTH halves of an offer.

  (a) AFFIX STARTING POWER LEVEL.
        unpromoted   new affix enters at level 1
        promoted X   new affix enters at level X + 1
      So promoted x5 picks a fresh affix straight into level 6, MASTERY.
      Re-picking the same card is still the other route up.
      WeaponInfo.zsc, RS_AFFIX_MAX_LEVEL = 6.

  (b) STAT CARD ROLL BANDS. Every stat card rolls a RANGE, and both ends
      shift up per promotion:
        Damage 2-4 (+1)   Accuracy 1.5-2.5 (+0.5)   Capacity 1 (+1)
        Crit .004-.007 (+.002)   Choke .04-.06 (+.01)
        Reload .04-.06 (+.01)
      Ceilings: relevant to none of the six. Damage's already scales off
      PromotionDamageBaseline; Accuracy and Condition are 0-100 by
      definition; Capacity and Crit have none; Choke 0.8 and Reload 1.4
      are mechanical bounds, not tier ones.

  (c) RELEVEL COST. 15% cheaper XP per promotion, capped at 60% off.
      WeaponInfo.zsc GetXPForLevel(). Flagged in-file as a placeholder
      curve pointing at rs_01.


3. THE PROPOSAL -- PROMOTION CHANGES WHAT A CARD *IS*   [NOT BUILT]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Owner, verbatim, so the intent is not paraphrased away:

    "when a weapon is promoted, it can get combo cards such as:
       promotion level 0 : card a - Dam + 3
       promotion level 1: card a - Dam + 3, Acc + 0..5
       promotion level 5: card a - Dam + 10, Acc + 2, Velocity + (0.3),
                          Reload Speed - 0.4 sec, Magazine +1

     we might even have affix cards change:
       promo level 0 - card b : Slugshot
       promo level 1 - card b: slugshot every other rnd, explosive
       promo level 5 - card b: slugshot every 3rd rnd. all critical shots
                       are plasma (visual type 7) with cryo damage,
                       secondary fire smoke grenade"

  SO THE IDEA IS: promotion does not merely scale a card's numbers, it
  adds CLAUSES. A card gets wider, not just bigger. At promotion 0 a
  combo card is one stat; at promotion 5 it is five stats. An affix card
  at promotion 0 is one behaviour; at promotion 5 it is a behaviour, a
  cadence rule, a crit rider and a secondary fire.

  WHY IT IS A GOOD IDEA, recorded so it is not re-argued: it makes the
  promotion loop legible. Today promotion pays out in numbers the player
  has to infer. Under this, a promoted gun's cards VISIBLY say more, and
  "promoted guns get truly wild" (rs_01's own phrase) becomes something
  you read on the card rather than something you take on trust.

  NOTE IT ALREADY HAS A FOOTHOLD: RS_Upgrade_Cards.zsc's combo-card
  header already says combo cards are "Offered only to promoted or
  high-tier guns; magnitudes scale with PromotionCount where damage is
  involved." The offering gate exists. What does not exist is clauses
  appearing.


4. THE OPEN QUESTION -- AND IT MUST BE ANSWERED FIRST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Owner: "i dunno how these get 1 - 6 power levels but i'm thinking out
  loud."

  That is exactly the right thing to be unsure about, because there are
  now TWO axes and they are not obviously compatible:

      LEVEL      1..6, earned by re-picking the same card. Owns MAGNITUDE.
                 Level 6 is Mastery -- a RULE change, never a bigger
                 number (rs_16).
      PROMOTION  0..N, earned by the Prototype -> Basic sacrifice. Under
                 this proposal it would own CLAUSE COUNT.

  Naively combined that is a 6 x 6 grid: thirty-six distinct printed
  states per card, times ~50 cards. Nobody is authoring that.

  THREE WAYS OUT, none chosen:

    A. PROMOTION SETS THE TEMPLATE, LEVEL SCALES IT. A card has one
       clause list per promotion band (say 0, 1-2, 3-4, 5+ -> four
       templates), and level multiplies every number in whichever
       template is active. Authoring cost: 4 variants per card, not 36.
       Cost: level and promotion can no longer be read independently
       off the card.

    B. CLAUSES ARE THE LEVELS. Drop the separate level axis for combo
       cards entirely -- clause count IS the power level, so promotion 5
       simply IS level 6. Cheapest to author and to explain. Cost: combo
       cards stop levelling by re-pick, which breaks the one rule every
       other card follows.

    C. PROMOTION UNLOCKS RIDERS, LEVEL POWERS THE BASE. The card's base
       clause levels 1..6 as today; each promotion permanently attaches
       one extra fixed rider that does not level. Keeps both axes honest
       and independent. Cost: the riders need their own small balance
       pass, and a promotion-5 card carries five riders whose combined
       weight has to be checked against rs_16's audit.

  MY READ, offered not decided: C. It is the only one where "what does
  level mean" and "what does promotion mean" both keep a single clean
  answer, and rs_16's five-test audit can still be run on a card without
  first asking which of thirty-six states it is in.

  WHATEVER IS CHOSEN, rs_16'S AUDIT STILL APPLIES: does a rolled stat
  stop mattering (dead), does one matter more (best), do two guns now
  play the same (dead), and never duplicate Promotion's own lane. A
  five-clause card is exactly the shape that quietly fails test 3.

  ALSO UNRESOLVED, from the owner's own example: "all critical shots are
  plasma (visual type 7) with CRYO damage". rs_16 BANS elementals as
  affix identities -- stock GunBonsai already owns fire/ice/poison/acid
  and this mod's identity is Doom's own guts. A cryo rider is either a
  deliberate exception to that law or it needs re-flavouring as kinetic
  or as a monster signature. That is an owner call and it is not made.
