PART 0 -- WHAT WE ARE ACTUALLY DOING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  IMPORTING COLOURFUL HELL. CH ONLY. NOT CHP.

  CH is one mod: 17 families x ~14 monsters = 242 creatures. That is the
  whole job.

  CHP is a SEPARATE, LATER job. It is an add-on that inherits CH's actors and
  re-stats each one into 15 strength copies (242 x 15 = 3,630). Building CH
  first is not just smaller, it is the correct order: CHP inherits FROM CH, so
  with CH right, CHP later becomes a stat overlay. The previous eight attempts
  built from CHP and used CH only to fill gaps, which is why our numbers are
  CHP's and our CH parent properties were missing.

  DO NOT OPEN CHP WHILE IMPORTING CH. If a value differs, CH wins, because CH
  is what we are building.


PART 1 -- THE SOURCE OF TRUTH IS THE WHOLE CH TREE, NOT ITS DECORATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  E:\New folder\ART SOURCE\CH\
      decorate\*.txt      the 17 family files -- the actors
      DECORATE.txt        SHARED actors: tier icons, gibs, common effects.
                          NOT optional. Easy to miss because the family files
                          look complete on their own.
      sounds\             785 lumps
      SNDINFO.txt         1,187 lines of definitions
      sprites\            8,965 files in 73 folders
      TRNSLATE.txt        palette remaps
      CVARINFO.txt        the player-facing toggles
      MENUDEF.txt         what those toggles are CALLED, and their values
      source\*.acs        the ACS -- see docs/rs_28_ch_acs_breakdown.txt

  A MONSTER IS NOT ITS ACTOR BLOCK. It is the actor PLUS its sprites, its
  sounds, its SNDINFO entries, its drops, its translation and its icons.

  THE CASE THAT PROVES IT: 87% of CH's sound library had never been imported.
  693 of 785 lumps and 804 SNDINFO definitions absent -- while every actor in
  the tree carried CH's sound STRINGS correctly. Eight passes copied
  `SeeSound "gen/see"` and nobody ever copied gen/see.

  It survived because AN UNRESOLVED SOUND NAME IS COMPLETELY INERT. No error,
  no warning, no log line. The compiler passes. The game runs. The monster is
  silent. There is no check that can fail -- the only detector is a human who
  already knows what it should sound like.


PART 2 -- FILE SHAPE. ONE CLASS PER CREATURE.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  zscript/monsters/<family>/RS_<Family>_<ROLE><nnnn>_<Name>.zs

  ROLE LETTERS -- a role means the same thing in all 17 families, whether the
  family has 13 creatures or 17. This is the whole point: `B0001` is the boss
  everywhere, where `case 11` was the boss in some families and mid-pack in
  others.

      C   common / vanilla-derived      (future vanilla-sprite imports land here)
      T   the CH ladder
      B   boss
      X   EX / megaboss                 (EX sits ABOVE both bosses -- verified:
                                         chaingunner 4500 / 6666 / 8999. NOT
                                         universal: revenant's EX is BELOW its
                                         second boss. Do not assume, read.)

  FOUR DIGITS, zero-padded. `TEX` IS RETIRED -- a letter tier is invisible to
  any numeric loop, and that exact bug shipped: a loop ran t = 1..12 and
  STRUCTURALLY COULD NOT SEE the EX tier, so a gibbed EX chaingunner played
  vanilla frames on the wrong body.

  Every class extends RS_MonsterMaster. Not Actor. Eight other systems read it
  (health bars, score, bits, elites, debug menu, catalog, keyword index,
  attack profiles) and they all break otherwise.


PART 3 -- THE ENGINE TRAPS. ALL OF THESE HAVE SHIPPED BROKEN.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. `r.flags` IN TierData IS MANDATORY.
     RS_ApplyTierProperties assigns flags ABSOLUTELY --
     `bAVOIDMELEE = (f & RS_TF_AVOIDMELEE) != 0;` -- and its own comment says
     the row is "a complete statement of the tier, not a delta". Leave r.flags
     at 0 and every flag in your Default block is SILENTLY STRIPPED at spawn.
     FLOORCLIP and MISSILEMORE are NOT managed by it and survive; do not put
     them in the word.

  2. THE `.T00` STATE ALIASES ARE LOAD-BEARING.
     RS_MonsterMaster dispatches every state through
     FindStateByString(prefix..".T00", EXACT). A plain `Missile:` label DOES
     NOT SATISFY AN EXACT LOOKUP -- MissileState resolves NULL and
     THE MONSTER NEVER FIRES A SHOT. Every file needs:
         Spawn.T00: Goto Spawn;   See.T00:    Goto See;
         Missile.T00: Goto Missile;  Pain.T00: Goto Pain;
         Death.T00: Goto Death;   XDeath.T00: Goto XDeath;
         Raise.T00: Goto Raise;
     ONLY for states the creature actually has.

  3. NEVER DECLARE Melee.T00 ON A MELEE-LESS MONSTER.
     A null MeleeState is what earns the engine's
     `if (MeleeState == NULL) dist -= 128` in P_CheckMissileRange. Declaring an
     empty one traps the monster at point-blank. Fixed family-wide once
     already; do not reintroduce it by "completing the set".

  4. `Game "Doom";` DOES NOT EXIST IN ZSCRIPT. DECORATE-only. Record it in the
     header comment, do not declare it. Five files failed to compile on this.

  5. NEVER FLATTEN A DAMAGE ROLL. `Damage (random(40,125))` -> `Damage 60` is
     silent data loss: no `random(` survives for any later sweep to find.
     `Damage (random(a,b))` in a Default block DOES NOT COMPILE -- that is the
     `damage: non-constant parameter` error. Use
     `DamageFunction (random(a,b));`, which compiles AND keeps the spread.
     Worse than it looks: a bare `Damage N` on a projectile is multiplied by
     random(1,8) by the engine and a DamageFunction is not, so flattening also
     inflates by ~8x at the top end.

  6. `Goto X+N` MUST KEEP ITS OFFSET, AND OFFSETS COUNT FRAMES.
     Rendering `Goto Missile+2` as `Goto Missile` has bitten this project
     THREE times. AND: deleting a 0-tic line (an icon spawn, an ACS call)
     SHIFTS EVERY OFFSET AFTER IT. If you drop a line inside a state that any
     `Goto X+N` targets, leave an explicit `TNT1 A 0;` placeholder and say so
     in a comment. One boss would otherwise have stopped rolling its attacks
     and lobbed the same bomb forever.

  7. ALWAYS `grep -i`. CH mixes `Actor` and `ACTOR`, `CallACS` and `callacs`.
     Case-sensitive searches produced FOUR confident false negatives in one
     session.

  8. ZSCRIPT CLASS NAMES ARE CASE-INSENSITIVE. RS_Foo and RS_foo are the SAME
     class and defining both is a fatal redefinition. Same for a subclass field
     shadowing a base field -- ZScript has no shadowing, so that is a
     redefinition too.

  9. DO NOT LINE-ANCHOR SEARCHES OVER CH. Most of it is inline one-line state
     blocks, so `^\s*THING` matches almost nothing. Strip `//` lines before any
     bulk pass or you will read prose as code.

 10. `static const TYPE name[] = {...}` DOES NOT RESOLVE on this build. Found
     three times in unrelated files. Use a switch or a `||` chain.

 11. A NEW .zs NOT IN zscript.txt SILENTLY DOES NOT EXIST. No error, no
     warning, the classes are just absent. Forgotten twice.

 12. ONLY ONE ACTOR MAY `replaces` A GIVEN CLASS. Moving a replacement means
     removing the old one in the SAME change.


PART 4 -- SOUNDS. THE ONE WITH NO ERROR MESSAGE.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * Import CH's SNDINFO entries AND the lumps from CH\sounds\.
  * INCLUDE THE `$` DIRECTIVES. `$random Foo { A B }` and `$alias` DEFINE
    names. A first pass here filtered out every line starting with `$` and
    silently dropped 88 definitions while believing it was finished. That is
    the same class of bug as the thing it was fixing.
  * FOLLOW THE CHAIN TO A REAL LUMP. `Science/Atk` -> `$random {Sci/atk1 ...}`
    -> `Sci/atk1  SCIAT1` -> the file SCIAT1.ogg. A `$random` pointing at
    undefined names is as silent as no definition at all.
  * Keep CH's sound STRING on the actor even when the lump is missing. An
    unresolved name is inert; deleting it destroys the only record of what the
    creature is supposed to sound like.


PART 5 -- NAMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A NAME COMES FROM CH OR THERE IS NO NAME. CH's own obituary, tag and actor
  names are fair game -- "The General", "Jetpack Larry", "Benellus", "Warface",
  "Moloch", "the crazy lady scientist" are all literally in the source.

  INVENTED BY PREVIOUS PASSES, ALL REMOVED: "STOMPER", "BLACK SHADOW BEAST",
  "SLIME GOLEM", "BLACK HADES", "the Smith". None appears anywhere in CH or
  CHP. A 20-line sweep over the source settles any name in seconds; do it
  rather than write flavour.

  AND: STOP NAMING TIERS BY COLOUR IN OUR OWN SYSTEM. The glow system makes
  four environment colours a live gameplay signal, so "green" becomes ambiguous
  between the monster and the light. Colour survives ONLY as the source
  citation in a header. Class names, filenames, docs and menus use role+number.

  Beware the double meaning that caused the worst defect in this project's
  history: in CH a colour is WHICH MONSTER; in CHP the same colour word is HOW
  STRONG. `GreenBlackCGuy2` is the BLACK monster at GREEN strength. Four
  families were ported off the wrong actor because of it, and it passed two
  review passes.


PART 6 -- WHAT "DONE" REQUIRES. EVERY LINE GETS A DENOMINATOR.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE OWNER'S DEFINITION, VERBATIM, 2026-08-05. THIS IS THE ONLY ONE THAT
  COUNTS, AND IT IS THE REPORT FORMAT:

      "get the chaingunner done" means
          VISUALS, SOUNDS, ATTACKS, BEHAVIORS, ICONS

  He had to say this more than once, and the reason is worth recording because
  it is a reporting failure, not a work failure: every progress report was
  written about WHICHEVER SLICE HAD JUST BEEN FINISHED -- "the bodies are done,
  the bullets aren't" -- instead of against the whole definition. Reporting a
  slice makes the OWNER do the integration and forces him to restate the goal
  every single time.

  So: report all five lines, every time, whether or not you touched them.
  A line you did not work on still gets its number. "ATTACKS: 0 of 38
  checked" is a report. Silence on attacks is how eight attempts looked
  finished.



  Before a family is reported at all:

      [ ] N of N CH bodies transcribed, each header citing its CH actor + line
      [ ] every sprite prefix present in sprites/            (count / count)
      [ ] every sound name resolves END TO END to a lump     (count / count)
      [ ] SNDINFO entries imported, `$` directives included  (count / count)
      [ ] DropItems carried, or itemised with CH line if the pickup class
          does not exist here yet -- never a silently gutted table
      [ ] TRNSLATE entry where CH sets one
      [ ] tier icons restored (see RS_ColorTierIcon.zs)
      [ ] all files in zscript.txt
      [ ] COMPILES -- zero script errors, from the engine, not from a script
      [ ] BOOTS, and every creature spawned and looked at in-game
      [ ] the differ run against CH and its output pasted

  NEVER WRITE "DONE". Write what was CHECKED and what was NOT OPENED. Those
  are different sentences and only the second one is honest. "Shotgunner: 14 of
  14 bodies state-diffed, 0 of 19 projectiles checked against CH, never booted"
  is a report. "Shotgunner done" is how eight attempts all looked finished.

  A NUMBER WITHOUT A DENOMINATOR IS NOT A RESULT.


PART 7 -- WHY EVERY CHECK THIS PROJECT EVER HAD WAS WORTHLESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  verify.py, verify_all.py, dedupe_check.py, crosscheck.py and
  build_art_index.py were deleted 2026-08-04. EVERY ONE OF THEM COMPARED THIS
  TREE AGAINST ITSELF. A lint over our own code cannot tell "faithful to CH"
  from "internally consistent and wrong", and the second is what kept shipping.

  A GREEN CHECK HERE HAS ONLY EVER MEANT "CONSISTENT WITH ITSELF."

  A tool is only allowed if it can say "CH's actor does X, ours does Y" and
  cite both files and both lines. chpdiff.py does this. And note what happened
  the first time it ran: it reported three mismatches and ALL THREE WERE BUGS
  IN THE DIFFER, accusing correct code -- a regex that parsed 1 of 14 rows, a
  positional off-by-one, and a lookahead one line too short. Hand-open every
  finding before believing it. Its first "323 of 333 match" was garbage.

  THE ONLY AUTHORITIES ARE THE COMPILER, THE RUNNING GAME, AND CH ITSELF.
  Including over this file.
