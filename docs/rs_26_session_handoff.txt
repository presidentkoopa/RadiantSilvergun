THE ONE THING TO UNDERSTAND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  EACH CHP TIER FILE HOLDS FIFTEEN ACTORS. The FIRST is Common<Something>
  and is the one we port. The other fourteen are the spawn-colour axis --
  Green, Blue, Purple, Yellow, Red, Black, White, Abyss, Brown, Cyan,
  Fireblu, Gray -- which rs_23 records as RECORDED, NOT BUILT.

  FOUR FAMILIES' TEX TIERS WERE PORTED FROM THE SECOND ACTOR.
  Found because the owner asked why Benellus had a green palette remap.

    02 Shotgunner   GreenWhiteSGEX2    02_WX:185
    09 Cacodemon    GreenBlackCacoEX2  09_KX:191
    03 Imp          GreenBlackImpEX2   03_KX:163
    04 Chaingunner  GreenBlackCGuyEX2  04_KX:114

  IT WAS NOT ONLY THE STAT ROW. Green numbers were baked into the state
  clusters too -- damage rolls, phase-2 health gates, A_SetSpeed values,
  melee rolls. And three families carried the green Translation:
  rs_sgun_tex, rs_imp_tex and rs_cgun_tex are BYTE-IDENTICAL,
  "0:255=%[0.00,0.00,0.00]:[0.18,1.32,0.18]", because all three were
  copied off their file's green actor. All three tint slots are now "-".

  HOW IT SURVIVED A FULL PORT AND TWO REVIEW PASSES: two of the four had
  headers that NAMED the green actor explicitly and listed its numbers
  correctly. The port brief named the wrong actor and the ports followed
  it honestly. Every header now names the Common actor.

  CLEAN, VERIFIED: Zombieman (the owner asked specifically -- its TEX is
  CommonBlackZombieEX2 and its tint slot is "-"), LostSoul, Demon (no TEX
  at all), Spectre, Revenant, PainElemental (no TEX), HellKnight,
  Arachnotron, Mancubus, Archvile, Baron, Mastermind, Cyberdemon.

WHAT ELSE LANDED TODAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * MeleeState/MissileState re-pointed per tier in ApplyTier. The
    dispatcher declared Melee: for EVERY monster, so MeleeState was
    non-null even on melee-less tiers -- costing them the engine's
    `if (MeleeState == NULL) dist -= 128` and trapping them at
    point-blank. Family-agnostic.
  * The CH parent property layer. Tier rows now carry flags (two words),
    species, body, scale, gibHealth, alpha/renderStyle, meleeRange,
    meleeThreshold, maxTargetRange, floatSpeed, radiusDamageFactor and
    noneDamageFactor. Tables written for families 01, 02, 03, 04.
  * rs_mon_missilechance_floor -- a valve under MissileChanceMult. CH's
    0.0625 (both missile flags) makes a monster stand still and hose;
    the row keeps CH's real value and the floor is applied at use.
  * Pain.AbyssPE moved to RS_MonsterMaster -- all seventeen families get
    the abyss conversion, because OUR conversion is always SetTier(6).
  * RS_BlackSGTrooper. The Crew Commander's squad was summoning four T07
    fireblu kamikazes; CHP summons a distinct actor with a five-stance
    tactical AI. Built.
  * RS_NoHitscan -- monster hitscans become travelling projectiles, with
    zero edits to the 163 hitscan call sites.
  * Green Benellus is no longer green (owner's call, and it turned out to
    be a fidelity RESTORATION, not a divergence).

DO THIS NEXT -- RANKED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1. IMP T12'S SUMMON SPAWNS THE WRONG CREATURE. Same class as the
     Shotgunner squad. We call SummonMinion("RS_Imp", -5) = a tier-7
     fireblu imp. CHP spawns SpecialImp2_C (03_W.txt:2455), a WEIGHTED
     RANDOM SPAWNER over five colours -- green 33%, blue 27%, purple 20%,
     yellow 13%, red 7% -- each overridden to Health 80 flat, RenderStyle
     Add / Alpha 0.95, +NOCLIP (they walk through walls), -COUNTKILL,
     Raise: Stop, a drop table, and A LEASH: beyond 800 units they
     A_Warp back onto the boss. Seven per cast.
  2. IMP T12'S MEGABALL FIRES NO MEGABALL. The HellionBall/Hel2 release
     is absent; the state fires only spark puffs. RS_HellionBall already
     exists at RS_hk_projectiles.zs:599.
  3. IMP T11 SpamShots is 5 of 12 balls -- waves 4-6 missing, including
     both wide spreads. IMP T08's command aura (Scatter) is missing
     entirely, though RS_BrownImpCommand already exists and is already
     wired into Demon and Spectre.
  4. FLATTENED DAMAGE ROLLS, family-wide. Chaingunner's are fixed; Imp
     has ~9 left in RS_imp_projectiles.zs. Remember WHY this is worse
     than it looks: a bare `Damage N` on a projectile gets multiplied by
     random(1,8) at impact and a DamageFunction does not, so flattening
     also inflates by roughly 8x at the top end.
  5. UNPORTED TIERS CHP SHIPS: 14_R2 CommonRedArch2 (1800/17/10 -- CH's
     RedArch is an ACS coin-flip between two monsters and we ship one),
     15_R2 CommonRedBaron1 (2400/10/20, distinct from the Fallen),
     05_K2, 12_W2, and the phase forms CommonWhiteArchEX3 and
     CommonWhiteCybieEX3.
  6. RS_EX_Baron's stats are invented -- CHP has NO Baron EX (15_KX and
     15_WX are both 2-byte stubs). Decide whether the class is
     intentionally RS-original and say so in the header; it currently
     claims it was "rebuilt from CHP's BlackEX/WhiteEX variants".
  7. TRANSLATION PARITY, 21 tiers in families 13-17. Their Common actor
     sets no Translation and therefore INHERITS a real CH palette remap,
     while our TintTable says "-". TRNSLATE.txt already holds unused
     transcriptions (rs_manc_*, rs_baron_*, rs_vile_*); rs_mind_* and
     rs_cyber_* were never written. NOT a mechanical flip -- the tiers
     whose Common actor writes `Translation None` or "0:0=0:0" are
     deliberately cancelling the parent and "-" is right for those.
     Needs an in-game look.

STANDING TRAPS CONFIRMED AGAIN TODAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * A ROW THAT OMITS A FIELD DOES NOT INHERIT CHP -- IT INHERITS US.
    Cyberdemon T05 carried painChance 20 from RS_Cyberdemon's own Default
    block; CHP states none, so the real value is 0. New trap, new shape.
  * `Goto X+N` rendered as `Goto X` has now bitten THREE times. Latest:
    RS_SpliceBaron looped to Missile+1 (a 20-tic A_FaceTarget) instead of
    +2 (the shot) -- quarter fire rate on a boss's phase-2 minion.
  * THE T00 FALLBACK FIRES FOR TIER 13. TierState falls back to
    "<prefix>.T00", and RS_AuditClusters loops t = 1..12, so it
    STRUCTURALLY CANNOT SEE TEX. A gibbed EX chaingunner was playing
    vanilla CPOS frames on an HCPO body.
  * A DEAD BRANCH IN CHP IS CORRECTLY ABSENT FROM OURS. Benellus's theme
    song has no jump into it -- the line is commented out in CHP itself.
    Check before "restoring" anything.

  Nothing in this session has been through a compile since the
  RS_BlackSGTrooper batch. That is the first thing to do.
