DESIGN LAW
================================================================================
L1. FULLSCREEN NATIVE MENUS ONLY -- every screen extends
    TFLV_Menu_GenericMenu (proven, boots today). No custom renderers.
L2. THE HAND LAW, honored by CONTEXT:
      * dual-hand screens (pickup compare): LEFT acts on offhand,
        RIGHT acts on mainhand.
      * single-subject screens (a sheet, a picker): Up/Down selects;
        Left/Right does that screen's one lateral action (reroll).
L3. BIND BUDGET (VR real estate): the existing cycle bind + Up/Down/
    Left/Right/Confirm/Back ONLY. No new keybinds anywhere.
L4. COLOR DOCTRINE (the anti-slop rule): screens are DOOM-toned --
    gold headers, tan/white body text, red warnings, the engine's own
    font palette. Rarity color appears ONLY as data (a tier word, a
    card border tag), never as decoration. No gradients, no glow-wash.
    Tier ramp (info-color, one place -- RS_UIStyle):
      Cursed=dark red  Trash=brown  Basic=grey  Common=white
      Uncommon=green   Advanced=blue  Designer=purple  Prototype=gold
    Mastery = orange, always and only.
    FUN comes from juice with intent: tier-colored weapon names, star
    pips for promotion (o o * * *), the orange Mastery moment, card
    flavor lines -- not from painting everything.
L5. ESC NEVER PUNISHES. XP-loss on reject: REMOVED (owner confirmed).
L6. DISCLOSURE TIERS. The sheet shows what changes decisions; the
    tooltip layer (already in the chassis) holds the depth. Nothing is
    hidden, but nothing shouts either.

STAT & COMBO CARD ROSTER (from RS_Roll + weapon stat model)
================================================================================
  Basic stat cards (green), always in the pool, ceiling-aware:
    DAMAGE +N        N scales with PromotionCount (the Discussion-A
                     rule: promotion makes picks BIGGER). Hard-capped
                     by GetDamageCeiling() -- its first real caller.
                     Card is simply absent while at ceiling.
    ACCURACY +2      cap 98.
    CAPACITY +1      no cap v1.
    CRIT +0.5%       no cap v1.
    CHOKE +0.05      shotgun-family archetype card only (Choke is
                     dormant on everything else -- verified).
    RELOAD TUNE +0.05  ReloadSpeed; tooltip explains bonus rounds.
  DELIBERATELY ABSENT: VELOCITY cards -- at rolled speeds the stat is
    perceptually nil (session finding); selling it as a card would be
    selling fake value. Returns if velocity scale is ever reworked.
  Combo cards (tier-colored purple/blue), offered only when
  PromotionCount >= 1 OR tier >= Advanced -- "promoted guns get truly
  wild" (owner, Q5). Two RELATED things, one card:
    MARKSMAN'S PACKAGE   ACC +3, CRIT +0.75%
    EXTENDED CHAMBER     CAP +2, RELOAD +0.05
    HOT LOADS            DMG +N, CHOKE +0.05 (shotguns) / ACC -2 (else)
    WINTER KIT           Cryo +1 level, CAP +1 (the affix-level-in-a-
                         card pattern -- requires Cryo held; one such
                         card per identity affix, same shape)
    TWIN DISCIPLINE      Splitter +1 level, ACC +2 (requires Splitter)
  Combo magnitudes scale with PromotionCount. This roster is the v1
  seed -- the pattern (stat+stat, stat+affix, affix+affix) is the
  point; more cards are content passes later.
