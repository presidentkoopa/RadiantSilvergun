GLOBAL POWER RULES (tuned against the monster ladder + rs_01 promotion math)
================================================================================
  * Everything multiplicative -- arsenal DamagePerShot spans 3..480.
  * A maxed (L5) affix stays within ~±30% net DPS. Promotions own
    tier-climbing (~1.9x per cycle, 1-3 monster tiers); affixes own SHAPE.
  * Mastery (6th take) changes a RULE, never the number budget.
  * Levels = GB re-takes (level field). Take 6 = Mastery. Cap enforced in
    IsSuitableForWeapon (GB convention -- no engine-side max).
  * OnActivate idempotent by reconstruction; OnDeactivate a complete undo
    (Promotion's affix strip depends on it -- RS_GunBonsaiBridge).
  * Exclusion groups: shape (Splitter<->Slugger), projectile identity
    (Cacospit<->Bonecaller<->PainOrb<->Arach-Plasma<->Swarm<->Nova).
    Everything else stacks across groups -- that's where builds live.
  * Wardrobe rule (owner-picked): part identities roll their look-FAMILY
    once at take-time (permanent for that gun), escalate WITHIN the family
    by level. First use: Cacospit's bile-ball/hell-wisp roll.

THE TEN (condensed sheets -- L1..L5 / MASTERY / gate)
================================================================================
01 SPLITTER   payload:split2..6, splitmaster
   N-way exact wash: xN fragments @ 1/N damage, N = level+1.
   MASTERY: deterministic even fan (no random scatter), cone +25%.
   Gate: bullet|hitscan beat. Excludes Slugger. Free (wash).
02 SLUGGER    payload:slug1..5, slugmaster
   Collapse volley to one round: dmg x1.8->2.6, spread x0.3->0.1,
   scale +10%/lvl. MASTERY: the slug pierces 2 bodies @ 80% retention.
   Gate: PelletCount >= 2, bullet|hitscan. Excludes Splitter.
03 OVERCHARGED payload:over1..5, overmaster
   dmg x(1+0.15L), spread x(1+0.20L) -- the sloppiness is the price.
   MASTERY (Focus): first shot after >=1s pause pays no spread penalty
   (RS_LastShotTic, stamped on every committed pull incl. backfires).
   Gate: primary beat SpreadScale > 0 (GH Railgun excluded -- its price
   would be counterfeit).
04 GIANT      payload:giant1..5, giantmaster
   Projectile scale x1.25->2.25 -- sprite AND hitbox (ApplyProjectileScale).
   MASTERY: Wrecking Ball -- rounds carry ProjectileKickback 600.
   Gate: bullet|heavy. Free -- identity/joke that's secretly a build.
05 SEEKER     behavior:seek1..5, seekmaster
   Homes TRUE at every level (owner's correction: never "gradually less
   blind"). Levels buy CYCLING: retarget when the victim dies, L-1 extra
   victims. Price -5% dmg/lvl. MASTERY: unlimited cycling + SMF_PRECISE;
   curves around corners (steering never needed line of sight).
   Gate: bullet|heavy.
06 GHOST      behavior:pierce1..5, piercemaster
   Punch through 1/2/3/4/all victims @ 70->90% damage retention per punch
   (DoSpecialDamage). MASTERY (stitch): a rip-kill flips the round into an
   unlimited precise seeker mid-flight. Gate: bullet only.
07 CACOSPIT   element:fire1..5, firemaster  [identity group]
   REFRAMED from the dead "Painter" elemental -- rs_11 killed elementals
   as a concept (stock GunBonsai already owns fire/ice/poison/acid, and
   this mod's identity is Doom's own guts). Same plumbing, new subject:
   rounds become the CACODEMON'S BILE -- wardrobe roll at take: the
   classic caco ball (vanilla BAL2 + caco/attack) or the hell-wisp
   (in-repo RSI1 living flame). +3% dmg/lvl. MASTERY: impacts leave
   burning bile (RS_AffixGroundFire via the ImpactSpawnExtra hook --
   the hook rs_06 flagged as missing now EXISTS, this is its first
   consumer). Keyword values still read "fire*": the axis is plumbing,
   not flavor.
08 BONECALLER behavior:bonechance25/50/75/100, bonemaster [identity group]
   Part-swap: Revenant bone tracers (vanilla FATB + skeleton audio -- zero
   asset risk). L1 pure identity; L2-4 per-PELLET hunt chance 25/50/75%;
   L5 all rounds hunt (chance 1.0 -- deliberately NOT plain "homing" so
   ungranting can never strip someone else's homing). MASTERY twin-fire:
   every round doubles into straight+seeker pair @ half damage. Exact wash.
09 PAIN ORB   element:ice1..5, icemaster  [identity group]
   REFRAMED from the dead "Cryo" elemental, same reasoning as 07. Now
   the PAIN ELEMENTAL'S OWN bouncing orb -- and it bounces from LEVEL 1,
   because the bounce IS the identity, not a reward for reaching six.
   Imported CH ice art (VIEWED before import; ICEY has no D/E frames --
   death anim is F..I, do not "fix" it to CDE). +6% dmg/lvl; price -1
   Capacity per 2 levels (restore-then-apply, drift-proof). Plain-Actor
   part (RS_AffixPartActor branch; FastProjectile cannot bounce, which
   is why that branch exists) -- the first bouncing player round in the
   project. MASTERY: bigger, six bounces, shatters into a shard spray.
   Keyword values still read "ice*" -- plumbing, not flavor.
10 ECHO       no keywords -- pure rotation
   First real consumer of the beat machinery (PadTo/Replace, built and
   never used). REWORKED under rs_11's audit: the original was a FREE
   fixed cluster beat, which ignored the Capacity roll and played
   identically on every gun -- an identity-test failure. The echo is now
   YOUR OWN SHOT DOUBLED at DOUBLE ammo cost: your roll amplified, paid
   for out of your magazine. N: 7/6/5/4/3 by level. MASTERY: every 2nd.
   Adopts only single-beat primaries (true of all 32 weapons today);
   restores the exact original profile object on removal.

LATER WAVES (built after this slate -- rs_11 is the law, rs_13 the survey)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAVE D1 -- MONSTER SIGNATURES (the exotic lane the elementals vacated).
  ARACH-PLASMA  element:arach1..5/master. The Arachnotron's bolt
      (APLS/APBX + baby/*, pure IWAD). The RATE signature: smallest
      damage ramp (+2%/lvl) because its identity is how fast it speaks.
      MASTERY: burns through two bodies @ 85% retention.
  SWARM         element:swarm1..5/master. The Overlord's bee carrier
      (LFX1 + WASP motes, both in-repo). Levels buy MOTES (2->6), not
      damage -- the carrier still lands the weapon's full rolled damage,
      closing the hollow-carrier trap rs_13 flagged. MASTERY: eight
      motes, hunting. Gated to <=3 pellets (engine load).
  NOVA          element:nova1..5/master. The Cyberdemon's swoosh shell
      (BFS1/BFE1/PLSS, all IWAD) detonating into a plasma bead ring.
      Levels buy weight (+4%/lvl) and beads (4->12). MASTERY: sixteen
      seeking beads. Gated to <=2 pellets; bead count hard-capped at 16
      in the part itself regardless of what the resolver asks for.

WAVE D2 -- GENERAL LANE (each amplifies a DIFFERENT rolled stat).
  PAIN TRAIN    behavior:flinch1..5/master. Per-pellet flinch chance
      15%->75%, guaranteed at Mastery. Plays against the monster
      ladder's painChance, which collapses at high tier (T12 = 16).
  MOMENTUM      payload:momentum1..5/master. Consecutive crits chain,
      up to 4 links (8 at Mastery). CritChance amplifier; the streak
      lives on RS_Weapon so each hand keeps its own rhythm.
  OVERKILL      no keyword -- OnKill hook. Damage wasted past a kill
      detonates on the corpse (20%/lvl, full excess at Mastery). Damage
      amplifier. The corpse is re-pointed at the player before
      detonating, so your own overkill can never hurt you.
  SCAVENGER     no keyword -- OnKill hook. Kills on a bottom-quarter
      magazine refund 10% of capacity per level, full mag at Mastery.
      Capacity amplifier.

  HOOK DISCIPLINE (rs_14's offhand findings -- binding on all future
  cards): use the resolver, OnKill, or OnDamageDealt. ModifyDamageDealt
  and Tick reach the MAINHAND bag ONLY, so a card built on them
  silently does nothing in the off hand.

  PROJECTILE IDENTITY EXCLUSION GROUP now spans all six: Cacospit,
  Bonecaller, PainOrb, Arach-Plasma, Swarm, Nova. One shared
  IdentityFree gate on the slate base -- a comparison chain, not an
  array (this engine does not resolve array literals in class bodies).

GENERATOR POOL (gap #1 from rs_06 -- CLOSED)
================================================================================
The original seven, all live: multi/cluster/slug (washes, 0) + homing (+0.5)
+ piercing (+0.5, bullet-mask) + sluggish (-0.1, bullet-mask) + wild (-0.5,

DECISIONS MADE DURING THE BUILD (so nobody re-litigates them blind)
================================================================================
  * BASE keywords no longer drive shot math (resolver reads GRANTED +
    beat-local only). This fixed every shotgun silently double-firing.
  * Bullet damage is EXACT now (GetMissileDamage override) -- the engine's
    stored x random(1,8) is gone from the ballistic tree. Heavy classes
    (Enhanced trio, GH shots) NOT yet converted -- their own pass, later.
  * Painter's fire sound is hq_dsfirsht for now (flamethrower loop reads
    wrong per-shot). Revisit during audio polish.
  * Bonecaller/Painter/Cryo deactivation nulls only its own installed
    parts (explicit class-lookup compares).
  * Ice import: 7 ICEY frames (A,B,C,F,G,H,I) + 4 CH oggs renamed
    ice_cast/hit/shatter/fly. VORTEX/ice_fly imported unused so far.
