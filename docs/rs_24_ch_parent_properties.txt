WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The owner's report: "they all seem to fire once while walking around,
  sometimes they fire a custom sprite, but the behavior doesn't seem too
  different... the behavior itself seems like just, zombieman."

  He was right, and every state block was correct. All fourteen Zombieman
  tiers were diffed against CHP line by line on 2026-08-05 and they MATCH --
  T01's double-shot and permanent gas walk, T06's fastchase interleave,
  T07's no-op Missile plus kamikaze Melee, T08's `See+3` re-entry, T10's
  `Missile2+1` loop that skips the wind-up. The port of the states is good.

  THE STATES ARE ONLY HALF OF A CHP ACTOR.

      ACTOR CommonRedZombie : RedZombie

  CHP files carry the STATES. Every combat PROPERTY -- flags, species,
  radius/height/mass/scale, damage factors, blood colour -- lives on the CH
  PARENT in CH/decorate/<Family>.txt. Four porting attempts read CHP and
  never opened the parent. Result: fourteen tiers running one identical
  property set, with only hp/speed/painChance varying.

  THE TWO THAT COST THE GRAMMAR:

    +MISSILEMORE  Halves the "don't fire" distance roll (modern spelling:
                  MissileChanceMult 0.5 -- LOWER FIRES MORE). CH puts it on
                  10 of 14 zombie tiers. Missing from all of them, so every
                  tier fired on the timid vanilla schedule. CH's design is a
                  CONTRAST -- T00/T06/T08/T10 cautious, the rest aggressive.
                  We had flattened all fourteen to cautious.

    +AVOIDMELEE   Keeps the monster at range instead of closing. CH puts it
                  on 9 of 14. Missing, so they walked into your face -- and
                  once there, the second defect below trapped them.

  AND A SEPARATE, COMPOUNDING BASE-CLASS DEFECT:

    RS_MonsterMaster's dispatcher declares `Melee:` for EVERY monster, so
    MeleeState was non-null on every tier of every family -- including the
    ones with no melee attack. Two consequences:
      1. P_CheckMissileRange does `if (MeleeState == NULL) dist -= 128;`
         -- "no melee attack, so fire more". Every tier lost that bonus.
      2. A_Chase checks melee FIRST and returns. At melee range a
         melee-less tier went Melee: -> TierState null -> Goto See ->
         chase -> Melee: ... and never reached its missile state.
    Of the 15 family-01 CHP actors only 01_F (T07) and 01_K (T11) define
    Melee:. The other thirteen have none, and get the -128.

  NONE of this is visible to any lint. It is the exact disease CLAUDE.md
  names: the tree was consistent with itself and wrong.

WHAT WAS BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  RS_MonsterMaster.zs
    * ApplyTier now re-points the state pointers per tier:
          MeleeState   = TierState("Melee");
          MissileState = TierState("Missile");
      TierState returns null when neither Melee.<tier> nor Melee.T00
      exists -- exactly the melee-less set. Family-agnostic.
    * RS_MonsterTierRow gained the CH property fields: flags, species,
      radius, height, mass, scale, gibHealth, bloodColor, missileChance.
    * RS_TF_* flag consts at file scope, named for what CH writes so the
      table diffs against CH/decorate without translation.
    * RS_ApplyTierProperties(row) applies them. FLAGS ARE ASSIGNED, NEVER
      OR'd -- the row is an absolute statement of the tier, so a flag T03
      sets is CLEARED when the monster becomes T04.
    * virtual TierDamageFactor(t, damageType) + a DamageMobj override.
      Per-type DamageFactors live on class DEFAULTS in ZScript and cannot
      be assigned per instance; this is the per-instance equivalent.

  RS_Zombieman.zs
    * All fourteen tiers' CH properties transcribed with parent name and
      Zombies.txt line number on every case.
    * TierDamageFactor override: DIMp 0 family-wide, Exorcist 3.0 on
      T00-T10, Heroic 3.0 on the three bosses, Fire/Melee 2.0 on cyan and
      gray, fire 0.25 on the fire zombie, Melee 3 on red, PlayerVoid 0.5
      and Falling 0.0 on TEX.
    * Mass and +ROLLSPRITE REMOVED from OnTierApplied. They were already
      correct there -- but OnTierApplied runs AFTER RS_ApplyTierProperties,
      so leaving them would have silently overwritten the row. One owner.

  DELIBERATELY RECORDED, NOT APPLIED:
    * bloodColor. It is a `color` field and setting it at runtime does
      nothing -- the engine bakes BloodTranslation at class init. The CH
      values are in the row because they are ground truth and finding them
      again costs a read of CH/decorate. Applying them needs a
      BloodTranslation rebuild, which is its own job.
    * +FLOORCLIP is NOT an assigned tier flag. Every CH parent has it and
      so does every Default here; making it absolute would silently clear
      it for the sixteen families with no table yet.

  WHAT T11/T12/TEX ACTUALLY ARE -- recorded because it surprised the owner
  and rs_23 did not say it plainly. Family 01 stops being zombiemen at the
  top. T11 is Player 9 (ZOMK, a dead marine), T12 is the UNDERTAKER (MAGE,
  a robed necromancer with a shovel), TEX is Player X (ZMKX). Only those
  THREE carry +BOSS -- T10 Red is not a boss, it is a nasty zombie that
  always gibs. All three bosses share one profile: +BOSS +QUICKTORETALIATE
  +LOOKALLAROUND +MISSILEMORE +NOFEAR +DONTMORPH -NORADIUSDMG. The
  -NORADIUSDMG is deliberate: CH wants them to take splash.

  T12's `Species "UnderTaker"` IS SHARED WITH MrBones. That is how the
  summoner does not shred its own skeletons. Drop it and the Undertaker
  kills its whole economy.

THE PLAN FOR FAMILIES 02-17
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THIS IS A READ AGAINST CH/decorate, NOT A REWRITE. For each family:
  open the CH file, extract the property block of each tier's parent, fill
  in a TierData property switch exactly as Zombieman's now reads. The
  state blocks are not touched.

  Measured scope -- actor counts include spawn-colour variants, so the
  number of PARENTS to read is roughly one per tier, not per actor:

    17 Cyberdemon       CYBIES.txt             140        15           0

  READ THE ZERO COLUMNS AS DATA, NOT AS ABSENCE. +AVOIDMELEE is a ranged
  attacker's flag -- the four families that have it are the four human
  gunner families plus the Pain Elemental, Archvile and Mastermind. A
  Baron does not avoid melee because a Baron IS melee. The distribution
  is the design; do not normalise it.

  +MISSILEMORE is on every family and is the single highest-value item on
  this list. 246 instances across the seventeen files, none of them ported.

  ORDER: rs_23's depth-first order still holds -- 02 Shotgunner, then 04
  Chaingunner, then 03 Imp. Shotgunner and Chaingunner share the human
  gunner shape with Zombieman, so their tables read almost identically and
  the diff is cheapest.

  DO NOT BULK-SWEEP THIS. The Zombieman table took a read of every parent
  with the line number written down next to each case. A mechanical pass
  that copies flags without opening both sides is the exact failure mode
  that produced four bad ports.

STILL OWED -- NEEDS ONE COMPILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Nothing in this batch has been through a build. The lines most likely to
  be rejected, in order:

    1. MeleeState / MissileState assignment in ApplyTier. If ZScript
       treats them as readonly on this engine, the -128 half cannot be
       fixed this way and the fallback (MeleeRange = -1000 on melee-less
       tiers) only kills the melee bounce, not the aggression loss.
    2. The DamageMobj override signature in RS_MonsterMaster.
    3. Species = r.species  (string -> Name conversion).
    4. bLAXTELEFRAGDMG, bQUICKTORETALIATE, bNOTARGETSWITCH spellings.
    5. RS_NoHitscan's AMRK sprite anchor -- the mod ships no sprite for
       it, so it resolves from the IWAD/gzdoom.pk3 or not at all. If it
       errors, any existing 1-frame sprite works; the model is what draws.
