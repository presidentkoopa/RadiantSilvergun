WHAT ZOMBIEMAN IS -- THE PROVEN METHOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Family 01 is ported and the owner has confirmed it works in game.
  It took: five agents reading CHP in parallel (three on tier ranges, one
  on ACS + a diff against our code, one broad), producing ~470KB of
  transcription with every claim citing CHP file and line; then me
  verifying citations and writing code from the merged result.

  A FAMILY OWNS ITS NAME IN ALL FOUR TREES:
      zscript/monsters/<Family>/RS_<Family>.zs
      zscript/monsters/<Family>/attacks/*.zs
      sprites/monsters/<Family>/T00..TEX
      docs/monsters/<Family>/
  The other sixteen stay FLAT until each is ported. Do not bulk-move
  them; a folder move makes the porting diff unreadable for no gain.

  WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG WITH ZOMBIEMAN, as a guide to what to expect:
    * The Undertaker's entire economy was missing. RS_MrBones existed in
      the tree and NOTHING EVER SPAWNED IT.
    * Its power ladder was fed from Pain -- damage TAKEN -- where CHP
      feeds it from skeleton DEATHS. Same variable names, inverted.
    * The ladder multiplied where CHP sets absolute values, so the final
      form was ~30% slower and the error grew every rung.
    * The Abyss tier's infection aura was gone entirely.
    * Pain.AbyssPE was missing from ten tiers AND its trigger was
      mistyped, so each half hid the other.
    * Eight damage rolls flattened to constants, one of them under a
      file header declaring it "house style".
    * The bone tornado had one orbiter type where CHP has seven.
  NOT ONE of those is a sprite problem, and none would be caught by any
  lint. They are all "the code is self-consistent and does the wrong
  thing".

DECISIONS MADE TODAY THAT SUPERSEDE OLDER DOCS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  * PROMOTION SETS AN AFFIX'S STARTING LEVEL: promoted X -> a new affix
    enters at X+1, so promoted x5 picks straight into level 6 MASTERY.
    Replaces rs_16's flat "+1, capped at L5". rs_16 updated.
  * PROMOTION WIDENS STAT CARD ROLL BANDS. All six cards, both ends.
  * SOCKETS are the affix BREADTH cap and nothing else. They are NOT
    "PACK sockets" -- that phrase was my drift and PACK is the eight
    PRESENTATION axes. An attack is an RS_AttackProfile in an
    RS_AttackSlot.
  * Bosses.acs and Announcers.acs are NOT PORTED -- 765 of 943 scripts,
    all HUD banners, one screen shake. The announcement layer belongs to
    the future SetPieces system and gets authored against OUR encounters.
  * The three empty per-family base classes are GONE. If family grouping
    is wanted, it is a Species string or a keyword axis, never a parent
    class that owns states.
  * The spawn-colour axis (15 sub-variants per tier) is RECORDED, NOT
    BUILT. Its Translation table is byte-for-byte identical across all
    fourteen tiers, so it is one table of 14 strings for a whole family.
