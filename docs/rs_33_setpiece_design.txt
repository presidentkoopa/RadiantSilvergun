================================================================================
rs_33 -- SETPIECE / CAPTAIN ENCOUNTERS -- CONSOLIDATED DESIGN OF RECORD
================================================================================
Assembled 2026-08-05 from three parallel design/audit lanes (spatial tech,
encounter design, adversarial integration audit) plus owner rulings given in
session. Nothing here is built except where marked BUILT. The Elite system
(rs_32, zscript/systems/monster/RS_Elite*.zs) is the substrate everything
below composes from.

The one-sentence concept, owner's own framing: encounters are sentences in a
grammar -- [monster tier] x [elite type loadout] x [command structure] -- and
a SetPiece is a purchased sentence. "It's the difference between a Prototype
drop and an Uncommon drop."

1. THE CAPTAIN FIGHT (chosen shape: B + E + F, with D as nightmare seasoning)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B  ROUND-ROBIN CONDUIT -- one tether burns hot at a time; damage dealt to the
   Captain lands on the hot conduit elite. Rotation every ~175 tics or on
   conduit death. Empty chain = Captain raw until the next gate re-forms it.
C  (cut -- pure-wager shape lost the flush moment)
E  STAIRCASE -- hidden squad elites reveal as the Captain crosses health
   quarters (75/50/25), each reveal announced in its type's pentagram color.
F  SEVERABLE TETHERS -- each tether carries a shootable KNOT; severing strips
   that elite's power off the Captain WITHOUT killing the elite. The elite
   goes feral (ordinary revealed elite, bits only), its package is FORFEIT,
   and (audit fix) the OFFICER'S package tier also steps down per sever --
   severing is a trade, never a discount code.
D  FIELD PROMOTION -- Grade III only: the dying Captain's soul flies as a
   shootable comet toward the nearest living unsevered elite. Intercept =
   fight ends now, +1 tier bonus. Miss = the elite becomes the new Captain
   and the fight continues, +1 tier to the eventual pot per succession.
   [Name collision with weapon Promotion flagged; owner may rename.]

THE FIGHT, BEAT BY BEAT (full detail in the encounter lane's report):
  0 CHALLENGE COIN -- gold buys a physical coin via the world-card vendor;
    the player CRUSHES it (VR grip) where they want the fight. Refusal in
    unfit rooms; coin not consumed on refusal.
  1 THE MUSTER (~8s) -- bell tone, light drop, room-scale pentagram
    (RS_ElitePentagram scaled up), trash muster arrives first, squad elites
    arrive hidden INSIDE the trash, Captain erupts last, tethers snap out,
    one burning. Fight grammar taught in one look.
  2 OPENING -- Captain revealed (boss-branch Reveal flags), one conduit
    revealed, reserves hidden on the staircase. TEMPO ORBS orbit the
    Captain (C07 leaf-orbit tech, shootable) -- see Stolen Time.
  3 DOUBLE-TIME -- every squad takedown puts survivors on ~2x speed
    (Grade III stacks to 3x) + the Reveal aggression flag set. ONE
    encounter-owned retimer, total factor clamped, floor max(1,tics) --
    never stacked on C04/C15/C17 carriers (audit: compounding + 0-tic
    defect class). 1.5s grace after each takedown.
  4 STOLEN TIME (R7-GATED: bullet time is opt-in, default off, consent
    cvar; fight must be fair with it OFF -- Double-Time tuned raw) --
    when enabled: takedowns 4s, orb busts 3s (glowing conduit orb 4s),
    gate breaks 2.5s of world-slow (world 0.30x, player 1.0x). Spend
    shooting the Captain, or sustain by scything trash (+1s/kill, orb
    chains); 10s cap, 75% chain fatigue. Grant windows defined in
    MAPTIME so the toggle cannot buy damage throughput (audit-verified:
    maptime runs during freeze; cadence is maptime-gated).
  5 THE KILL -- chain empty, quarters spent, Captain dies elite-style
    plus reverse-Muster; fixed 5s ceremony slow (not extendable).
  6 MUSTER PAY -- killed-unsevered elites re-ignite in their type color
    and their packages rise; the Captain's package descends center-sigil
    with tier announced as orbiting rings (1=Cursed..7+crown=Prototype).
    Bits shower contains NO gold (anti-laundering invariant). R3 card
    rules apply: no banking, rejected cards persist, recycle pays gold.

2. SPATIAL TECH (verified against the fork's source; full citations in the
   spatial lane's report)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROOM SCAN (purchase-time only, never per tic): 16 LineTrace rays at 22.5
degrees (TRF_THRUACTORS, z+32), r_free = 4th-smallest distance, corridor
axis test, median headroom via sector planes. Classification:
  corridor < 176 free / small 176-319 / arena >= 320 (headroom >= 120).
All thresholds cvars (rs_setpiece_*).

PLACEMENT: the engine's own summon idiom (Hexen Dark Servant: spawn the
real actor, TestMobjLocation, destroy + REFUND on failure) -- gold is never
taken unless the whole squad placed. Ring layout: Captain forward at
0.6*r_free, elites at +/-70 and +/-140 (nothing dead-behind: VR courtesy).
Per-candidate: IsPointInLevel -> same-floor rule (2x MaxStepHeight) ->
spawn-probe-destroy -> CheckSight gate. Shrink ladder 1.0/0.8/0.64 with
angle jitter. All-or-nothing; Captain places first.

STAGING: spawn parked (the C01 hiding flag set -- known good), gold sigils
+ TeleportFog + incoming sound, unpark after 35 tics with re-validation,
face player, target player, second fog.

REFUSAL: the summoning circle draws at the player's feet AT THE SIZE THE
ROOM ACTUALLY OFFERS, dull red, sputters, dies + refuse sound. Wordless.

CONTAINMENT: no arena locks ever (softlock-proof by construction). Soft
leash: >~1200u from squad centroid (Level.Vec2Diff -- portal-aware) for
~10s = reverse-Muster collapse + partial refund (~50%, cvar). v2:
Captain-only teleport-tether pursuit before collapse. Doorway cheese:
Captain gets the C06 no-LOS blink (shipped tech); unengaged Captain
regens 5%/s; conduits leash 512u to the Captain.

DIRECTOR SHAPE: RS_SetPieceHandler (EventHandler, netevent entry
rs_setpiece_buy/scan/test; MAPINFO-registered) + RS_SetPieceController
(Thinker per live encounter: Staging->Live->Complete/Collapsed) +
RS_SetPieceToken (membership marker) + encounter catalog as a switch.
File: zscript/systems/encounter/RS_SetPiece.zs (bucket placement =
owner's call). Four-point wiring per the standing checklist.

3. AUDIT LEDGER -- must-address findings (adversarial lane; build gates)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1 DESIGN CONTRADICTION: pure conduit redirect means Captain health never
   moves, so staircase gates never trip. RESOLVE BEFORE BUILD: partial
   passthrough (recommended ~20-25% reaches the Captain -- also gives the
   health-bar system a live pacing bar) or pooled encounter health.
   OWNER CALL (a).
A2 REDIRECT TOOL: Inventory.AbsorbDamage on the Captain (fork calls it for
   monsters, pre-application) -- zero there, DamageMobj the conduit with
   ORIGINAL INFLICTOR + PLAYER AS SOURCE. Never refund-and-redeal from
   WorldThingDamaged: it double-fires damage events = silent 2x GunBonsai
   XP farm + doubled affix procs. The source pointer is load-bearing:
   source=Captain would zero all GunBonsai XP and drop score to the 0.2x
   infighter consolation for the whole fight.
A3 DESIGNATION IS THE REVEAL: an elite becomes conduit -> tok.Reveal()
   fires. Never routes forced damage into a hidden elite (which would
   forfeit its drop with zero player agency), the full-heal becomes the
   "conduit charging" tell, and the routing target is legible in VR.
A4 SPAWN-ROLL RACE: WorldThingSpawned's ambient 5% roll fires DURING
   Actor.Spawn -- squad members can be claimed with wrong types. Fix:
   spawn bFRIENDLY (roll skips friendlies) -> mark -> unflip. Every
   SetPiece actor that must never roll carries RS_EliteNullToken.
A5 TOKEN AUTO-REVEAL vs STAIRCASE: the 50% auto-reveal races the gates;
   add a suppress bool on RS_EliteToken (small additive edit, my lane).
A6 SCORE LAUNDERING: gold->SetPiece->score->extra lives crosses the walled
   currencies. SetPiece monsters carry a marker; RS_Score zero-rates them
   (Bits still pay -- part of the purchased reward). Note IsSummonedMinion
   can't cover this (squad are vanilla actors).
A7 SAVESCUM: roll the ENTIRE payout at purchase, store on the controller;
   the kill only delivers. Post-purchase saves already contain the outcome.
A8 MULTI-POWER CAPTAIN IS A REFACTOR, NOT A LOOP: token holds one
   colorId/controller; handler missile-riders and on-hit payloads switch
   on one id; HP mults compound (C02xC17xC15 = 20x); C07+C16 opposed
   thrust on the player is a VR comfort violation; C01 BUDDHA deadlocks
   other death paths. v1 Captain wears EXACTLY ONE power. Multi-power =
   controller array + handler loops + compatibility matrix, week-class.
A9 BULLET TIME ENGINE FACTS (for the later opt-in integration): native
   TimeScale exists but is console-only, netgame-blocked, and slows the
   avatar against 1:1 VR hands -- unusable. PowerTimeFreezer is FREEZE;
   maptime keeps running during freeze (all cadence/beep timers proceed).
   Revealed elites carry bNOTIMEFREEZE (their bodies act at full speed
   while their controllers pause on isFrozen) -- an integration pass must
   reconcile, or the paid encounter is immune to its own slowdown.
   Duty-cycled freeze is the only mechanism that gets projectiles right;
   glow publishers strobe under it (per-tic republish) and need exemption.
   Score spree timers + Condition hazard grind run in real time under
   freeze -- both need one-line isFrozen skips when BT ships.
A10 BUDDHA/banked lives: a SetPiece cannot kill a banked player -- tune as
   a LIVES TAX, and remember each burned life hands ~2s invulnerable
   burst. Abandon-regen (no contact ~45s = squad heals full, staircase
   resets, nothing despawns) closes flee-and-heal.
BUILT SAME DAY: C04 Yellow's missing retime floor (0-tic states) fixed in
   RS_EliteColors.zs -- the audit's one live-code find.

4. GLOW CHOREOGRAPHY (owner direction 2026-08-05; gitd-lane consult PENDING
   on capabilities -- fold answers here when they arrive)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Owner: "it is a great way to control color, and take advantage of glow,
shapes, monster tiers, elite profiles, and fun custom group behaviors to
keep the player on their toes." The glow/billboard primitives become the
encounter's choreography layer:

  PROMOTION STATIONS -- a glow shape on the floor monsters RUN TO; crossing
  or standing in it tiers them up / re-colors them. SetPiece use: the
  Muster sigil doubles as a station the trash tries to reach -- every
  grunt that crosses gets a tier or a type tint. The subgame: intercept
  the runners before they cross. (Group behavior: the squad HERDS trash
  toward the station.)
  PAINTER LINES -- elites draw type-colored lines on the floor; a monster
  crossing a line takes that color/type/buff. C17's Midas as a line
  instead of an aura; C05/C14 creep trails already ARE proto-painter
  lines. Player counter-play: cut the line's painter, or bait crossings.
  THE 4x8 GLOW AXES -- owner's phrase for the glow system's structure;
  encounter logic reading/writing those axes at runtime to choreograph
  combat FLOW: lanes of danger, safe channels, pulse rhythms that
  telegraph waves, gradients that herd player or monsters. Awaiting the
  gitd lane's answer on what is readable/writable per tic.
  ENTRY METHODS (same consult): glowspots that saturate and birth
  monsters; reversed-deathfx arrivals (assembling from gibs); BILLBOARD
  GATES -- a standing billboard plane as a portal monsters step through;
  ceiling drops / floor eruptions. Summon-ceremony options list.

Grades scale choreography: Picket = sigil only; Honor Guard + painter
lines; Dead Detail = full flow choreography.

5. V1 -- MINIMAL SHIPPABLE (audit lane's cut, adopted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Buy (netevent + gold check + fit test BEFORE spend) -> summon (friendly-
flip marking; Captain = ONE forced type via the ForceElite seam, revealed;
2 revealed clean-type squad elites -- C05/C08/C10/C13 have no death-path or
retimer entanglements) -> fight (no conduit, no staircase, no tethers, no
escalation, no bullet time; C06 blink anti-doorway; abandon-regen) -> pay
(pre-rolled package delivered on Captain death via marker).
Bookkeeping: RS_SetPieceToken + score zero-rate + RS_EliteNullToken.
Estimate: 1-2 build sessions + 1 tuning. NOTE: the SetPiece package is the
FIRST implementation of the weapon-drop payout -- the elite drop system
(rs_32 s.7) should land first or together.

RETURN ORDER for cut pieces, each gated on the last:
  1 escalation (one clamped encounter retimer)      1-2 sessions
  2 staircase + passthrough + reveal-suppress flag  2-3 sessions
  3 round-robin conduit (AbsorbDamage, A2/A3 rules) build 1 session,
    attribution play-verification is the real cost  ~week honest
  4 tether knots + sever scaling                    2-3 sessions
  5 multi-power Captain (A8 refactor)               ~week
  6 bullet time integration (A9, R7)                last, ~week

PREREQ SEAM (my lane, one method, owner nod needed): public
RS_EliteHandler.ForceElite(mon, typeId, revealNow) -- strips any existing
token/controller, re-marks with the forced type. Everything in v1 needs it.
