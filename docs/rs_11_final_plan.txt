The binding design law for the affix/content system, written after the
ten-card under-delivery and the owner's corrections. Supersedes rs_09's

THE TWO LAYERS -- never confuse them again
================================================================================
  WEAPON STATS -- 12, rolled/derived/assigned (rs_00): DamagePerShot,
    RateOfFire, TimeBetweenShots, DPS(derived), Accuracy, Velocity,
    CritChance, Capacity, GunBonaiSockets, Condition, PelletCount,
    Reserved. THE GUN'S SOUL. Affixes may read and amplify these,
    never erase them.
  PACK INGREDIENT AXES -- 8 per bullet profile (rs_05): Projectile,
    Casing, Muzzleflash, Barrel Smoke, Firing Sound, Puff, ImpactFX,
    Trail. THE ATTACK'S BODY. Affixes edit these axes and the
    rotation; the catalog supplies every value.

THE AUDIT -- every card passes or dies
================================================================================
  1 IDENTITY   does any rolled stat stop mattering?          -> fail
  2 AMPLIFIER  does some rolled stat matter MORE?            -> best
  3 CONVERGENCE do two different guns play the same with it? -> fail
  4 GENERATIVE  similar / opposite / synergistic / antagonistic asked
  5 OWNERSHIP  never duplicate Promotion's lane (pellet growth)
  Standing verdicts: Echo reworked (own-shot echo, primes on full mag);
  Seeker turn-rate scales with rolled Accuracy; Ghost retention scales
  with rolled Velocity; elementals (Painter/Cryo) DIE as concepts,
  plumbing re-aimed at Monster Signatures.

MODE IS PRESENTATION (the railshot principle)
================================================================================
  A "hitscan" may really be a near-instant segmented projectile --
  hitscan feel, projectile freedoms (arc, seek, visibility). The
  catalog + profile system can represent mechanics the engine "lacks."
  This is the license for every exotic affix.

  Cursed/Trash/Basic  2 cards, STAT ONLY. Ranges scale with
                      PromotionCount; ceilings real (Accuracy & Condition

  Common              [FLAG for owner: sockets begin here (1) per rs_00,

            built late, behind its own gate, felt before more exist.

STAT-STATE VISUAL LADDER (owner directive, build committed)
================================================================================
  The gun's LOOK reflects its stat state, using the catalog:
    Damage vs ceiling:  LOW / MID / HIGH / PEAK -- hotter, brighter
                        rounds as damage climbs (RSB0 base -> ember ->
                        flare-shedding peak).
    Condition bands:    clean / worn / failing -- dimmer rounds,
                        sputter trails, heavy muzzle smoke as the gun
                        wears.
  Applies to DEFAULT bodies only -- never overrides an installed affix
  part; trail/smoke/puff state effects compose with everything.
  Sprites verified in-repo: RSB0, RSP0-6 plasma, RSL0 lightning, RSK0
  smoke, RSS spark motes.

THE TELL SYSTEM (cadence feedback -- the missing audiovisual language)
================================================================================
  HARD CONSTRAINT (owner): VR, 3D weapon models, NO weapon sprites --
  nothing may be drawn on the weapon or screen. Verified compatible:
  the whole FX tree is already world-space actors (MuzzleEffects/
  CasingEject/RS_MuzzleLight -- zero PSprites anywhere). The tell is
  therefore AUDIO-FIRST: hammer-click (revolver), pump-clunk (shotgun),
  bolt-snap (rifle), hiss (SMG), beep (plasma -- the owner's own
  prototype), capacitor whine (rail). Optional second channel: a dim,
  cool-toned WORLD-SPACE light pulse at the weapon (an RS_MuzzleLight
  cousin, clearly not a muzzle flash, same performance cap). The ready
  instant is exactly readable today: NextFireTic + the semi-auto
  release gate -- a Ready-state watcher fires the tell once per cycle.
  Teaches cadence with zero UI; gives rhythm affixes a beat the player
  can HEAR; differentiates archetypes on every single trigger pull.
  Two-stage reload audio where the weapon's action warrants it.
  SOURCE LIBRARY (verified): E:\New folder\li-gnrcwpn\ -- FOLEY1-5,
  HOLSTR set, per-archetype equips (RFLEQUP/SGEQUP/RLEQUP/MGUPSN/
  PLSUSND), per-weapon handling dirs with slide/pump/click/reload
  already SNDINFO-mapped. Import per the ICEY discipline: listen/view,
  categorize into sounds/combatfx/handling/, entries in our SNDINFO.
