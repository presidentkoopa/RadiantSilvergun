SURVEY NOTES (decisions owed to the main thread, not made here):
  F1  BEEP SPAM ON FULL-AUTO: DoEffect fires AllClear every time maptime
      reaches NextFireTic -- during HELD full-auto fire that is every
      cycle (35x/sec on the GH Minigun), a beep stream fighting the fire
      sound. Suggest: suppress AllClear while the trigger is held on
      trigger:fullauto weapons -- beep only when the gate reopens after a
      release or an empty-mag pause. That also makes AllClear mean
      something on these guns ("you may resume") instead of narrating.
  F2  RS_GH_PumpShotgun is archetype:shotgun but has NO pump between
      shots (Shoot: HBSG B/C/D, no action call) and a SILENT atomic
      9-shell reload (SSHR A-I, no sound). The archetype exclusion
      leaves it the one genuinely mute weapon in the arsenal. Fix is
      audio, not a beep: give its reload the already-imported pump set
      (rs_vp_shotgun_pump, rs_vp_shotgun_bck/fwd, rs_vp_shotgun_load).
  F3  RS_GH_AssaultShotgun is archetype:shotgun but trigger:fullauto,
      RoF 5, atomic 12-shell reload -- no per-shot action exists or is
      possible. Either it inherits the shotgun NO by archetype (current
      committed behavior) and gets C2 reload audio only, or it becomes
      the one shotgun with a beep. Flagged, not decided.
  F4  Fire-reload-fire weapons confirmed by state-machine read:
      VR_Shotgun (pump cycle inside Shoot: shotpump + shotcycle +
      shotload every shot -- action-tell TRUE today), VR_SuperShotgun
      (Shoot falls through to Reload; engine A_OpenShotgun2/Load/Close
      play built-in audio -- TRUE today), RS_GH_SSG (Capacity 2, must
      break open after every discharge, but its SHTZ A-Z reload plays
      NOTHING -- action-tell only becomes true when C2 wires
      rs_vp_ssg_opn/load/cls into it). RS_GH_HandGrenade is feed:none
      (no reload state at all); the launcher beep covers it.
